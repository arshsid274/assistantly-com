Where does Assistantly source its talent?

Assistantly works with offshore professionals primarily from the Philippines and Latin America. Both regions are known for strong English proficiency, cultural compatibility with Western business practices, and a deep talent pool of skilled professionals across many disciplines.

What roles can I hire through Assistantly?

You can hire across Admin, Operations, Marketing, Customer Support, Finance, and niche roles.

How fast can I hire a Unicorn?

On average, we place Unicorns in 14 days or less.

How does pricing work?

Most roles are $2,800–$3,000/month, while niche or highly specialized roles start at $3,000+/month. No hidden fees.

What if my Unicorn isn’t the right fit?

We offer a risk-free replacement guarantee.

How do you ensure Unicorns are the right match?

We use our proprietary Leadership DNA™ Profile to align each Unicorn with your leadership style and culture.

How do I know I can trust the candidates?

Every Unicorn goes through skills assessments, cognitive + behavioral testing, and soft skills screening. Their performance is monitored by Success Managers, with full visibility in the Assistantly Dashboard.
