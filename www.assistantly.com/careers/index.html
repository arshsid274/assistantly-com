A Process Built to Find Great Talent

Our Hiring Journeying
1.
Application

Tell us about your experience and your goals.

2.
AI Interview

Info gathering, knockout questions, role alignment.

3.
Recruiter Screening

A human review of your experience, communication style, and readiness for Unicorn-level work.

4.
Live Interview

A real-time conversation to assess problem-solving, professionalism, and how you show up in the moment.

5.
Cognitive & Behavioral Testing

Insight into how you think, work, communicate, and make decisions under real-world conditions.

6.
Unicorn DNA Profile Build

We map your strengths, delegation style compatibility, and working preferences to build your unique Unicorn profile.

7.
Role Endorsement

Final approval confirming you’re aligned, vetted, and ready to be matched with the right client.
