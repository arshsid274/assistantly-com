A Different Kind of Staffing Story

Assistantly began in 2020, founded by a young entrepreneur from an immigrant family who saw just how broken the offshore staffing world truly was.

Most offshore professionals faced:

💸    Low pay, no benefits
⏰    Long hours, little recognition
🏭    Factory-style “churn and burn” work environments

PLAY
PLAY

We set out to change that.

Bootstrapped from day one, Assistantly has grown into a global company serving hundreds of businesses and thousands of Unicorns across the Philippines
and LATAM.

We’re proud to be people-centric, relationship-driven, and obsessed with building the best offshore staffing experience in the world.

Happy Unicorns
means Thriving Clients

At Assistantly, Unicorns come first. Why? Because when they’re supported, motivated, and fulfilled, they deliver unmatched results for our clients.

Here’s what that looks like:

Fair & Aspirational Pay

We ask every Unicorn what they want to earn — not just enough to survive, but to save and thrive.

Benefits That Matter

Health allowances for families, profit sharing, and resources that many in PH + LATAM don’t otherwise have.

Recognition & Bonuses

Monthly all-hands since day one (never skipped) to celebrate top Unicorns, share wins, and stay connected.

Access & Support

Every Unicorn gets a dedicated Success Manager, plus direct access to our exec team. You’re never a number here — you’re a name.
